Cloud and remote access are additive — never load-bearing. If the internet drops, your pool pump still runs on schedule. Your tank still gets monitored. Your inverter data still logs locally. We call this local-first. In South Africa, we consider it non-negotiable.

Philosophy

Your systems should work whether the cloud does or not.

Most "smart home" products stop being smart the moment the internet drops or the company's server goes down. SI products don't. Every edge device retains autonomous control — locally, without phoning home.

Remote access is available and easy. But it is never required for your systems to function. In South Africa, with unreliable connectivity and rolling load shedding, that is the only responsible design choice.

Energy Intelligence

Meet ECM —
Energy Comfort Model

Most solar systems flood you with raw numbers — watts, SOC percentages, import/export figures. ECM distills all of that into a single live score that answers the only question that matters:

"How energetically comfortable is my property right now?"

ECM Score 0 — 20
Critical Cautious Moderate Comfortable Excellent
⚡
Combines six live signals

Solar surplus, battery state, grid dependence, load demand, stability and system headroom — weighted into one score.

🤖
Drives smart automation

SI Core uses ECM to decide when to run discretionary loads — pool heating, geysers, EV charging — without you lifting a finger.

📡
Available everywhere

Exposed via MQTT, Home Assistant and the SI app — so your dashboards and automations always have a single source of truth.
