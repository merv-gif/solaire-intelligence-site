Dongle offline?

Sunsynk dongle not connecting or not reporting?

Before you replace anything, check the usual causes. A weak WiFi signal at the inverter, a new router or changed WiFi password, the dongle's upload interval, and outages on the manufacturer's cloud all show up the same way: an app that stops updating, or data that arrives in patches.

  • Check the WiFi signal where the inverter is mounted. Garages and outside walls are common dead spots.
  • If you changed your router or WiFi password, the dongle needs to be paired again.
  • Check whether the manufacturer's app is down for everyone, not just you.
  • Ask your installer to confirm the dongle's upload interval.

If you're done depending on a cloud you don't control, the SI Gateway reads the inverter directly and keeps your data on your own network.
