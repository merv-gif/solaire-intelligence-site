How it fits together

Two components. One seamless system.

SI Gateway bridges your inverter to the rest of the SI ecosystem. It reads raw inverter data via RS485, calculates the ECM score, and exposes it via a local HTTP endpoint — which every connected SI device polls directly. Dashboard and Home Assistant telemetry flows over MQTT; SI devices use HTTP and don't depend on a broker at all.

Capabilities

Not just a data logger.

☀️

Real-Time Inverter Telemetry

Solar PV output, battery SOC, grid import/export and load — live on your dashboard. Every value that matters, always current.

🔒

Local-First Architecture

Dashboard and device logic run on your local network. No cloud account required, no internet dependency for monitoring, control or automation.

🌐

Remote Access Without Port-Forwarding

View your system from anywhere via an encrypted managed tunnel. First 12 months included. No router configuration. No third-party tunnel service.

🏠

Home Assistant Compatible

Every inverter entity exposed via MQTT. Solar, battery, grid and load available in HA the moment a broker is configured — no custom integration required.

🔗

Multi-Gateway Support

Multiple SI Gateways per account — for larger sites, multi-phase systems or properties with more than one inverter. Each Gateway pairs independently.

📈

Historical Analytics

Log inverter performance over time. Review daily solar yield, battery cycles, grid dependency and load patterns. Identify inefficiencies and anomalies across weeks and months.

🛠️

Remote Support Layer

Installers can access the system remotely for configuration, diagnostics and troubleshooting — with customer permission. Reduces callout costs for both parties.

🧠

Ecosystem Orchestration

SI Gateway is the coordination layer for the whole SI platform. It aggregates telemetry from SI Pool, SI Water and future products — enabling cross-domain automations like battery-aware heating and solar-surplus pool heating.

🔌

Prepaid Electricity Tracking

Log your prepaid balance and SI Gateway tracks remaining units against your actual consumption. Know your days-to-empty before your CIU does — with low-balance alerts and one-tap recalibration from your meter.

Prepaid electricity

Know exactly how many days of electricity you have left.

Log your prepaid balance once and SI Gateway does the rest — tracking your consumption against your units in real time. Based on your 7-day average, it tells you exactly how many days remain before you need to top up.

When you buy more units, tap Add Units and enter the kWh from your token slip. If the estimate drifts, Recalibrate from your actual CIU meter reading to snap it back to reality.
