SI Gateway
The inverter bridge — one per site

Connects your inverter to your intelligence layer.

SI Gateway is a compact RS485-to-Wi-Fi bridge that sits in your distribution board or next to the inverter. It reads live Modbus data from your Sunsynk inverter and relays it to SI Core over your local network — giving you real-time solar, battery, grid and load data without any cloud dependency.

SI Gateway also hosts the ECM (Energy Comfort Model) — a derived score that combines solar surplus, battery state, grid dependence and load demand into a single live number that tells you how energetically comfortable your property is right now.

✦
Live inverter telemetry

Solar PV, load, grid import/export, battery SOC and charge/discharge — polled every few seconds via Modbus RS485.

✦
ECM Score

A live 0–20 energy comfort score that drives SI Core automations — no manual rules needed.

✦
Inverter controls

Set SOC targets per time slot, configure work mode, grid charge and solar sell — all from the app.

✦
Pairs in minutes

Installer provisions via QR code. No port forwarding, no cloud account required for local operation.

SI Pool
Smart pool controller — one per pool

Intelligent pool control, from filtration to heating.

SI Pool replaces the old timer-and-floats world with a calibrated controller that combines temperature sensing, pump control, energy metering, filter health tracking and dry-run protection in a single installer-friendly unit.

When paired with SI Core and the ECM score, SI Pool can automatically run filtration and heating during periods of solar surplus — reducing grid consumption without any manual scheduling.

✦
Temperature monitoring & control

Live pool temp with configurable target — SI Pool manages heating automatically to hit your setpoint.

✦
Pump scheduling & energy metering

Two independent timer windows, live power draw in watts, voltage and current — full visibility of pump energy use.

✦
Filter health & dry-run protection

Filter clean threshold configurable in watts. Dry-run detection with grace period protects the pump from running without water.

✦
Remote control

Turn the pump, light and heat pump on or off from the app — whether you're at home or away.

SI Water
Tank & borehole monitor — one per tank

Know exactly what's in your tank, right now.

SI Water uses an ultrasonic sensor to measure your tank level continuously and reports volume in litres, height in centimetres and percentage fill — live to the app, with no float switches, no mechanical parts and no guessing.

Flow meters track inflow and outflow in real time, log usage events and calculate daily, monthly and all-time totals. Useful for spotting leaks, understanding consumption patterns or simply knowing if your borehole is keeping up.

✦
Ultrasonic level sensing

Continuous measurement in litres, cm and % — calibrated to your specific tank dimensions.

✦
Live flow monitoring

Net flow, inflow and outflow in litres per minute — updated live. Spot a leak the moment it starts.

✦
Usage totals & event log

Today, this month and all-time in/out totals. Recent water events log every fill and draw cycle with volume.

✦
Low-level alerts

Push notification when tank drops below 20% — so you're never caught without water.
