SI Gateway
The inverter bridge — one per site

Connects your inverter to your intelligence layer.

SI Gateway is a compact RS485-to-Wi-Fi bridge that sits in your distribution board or next to the inverter. It reads live Modbus data from your Sunsynk inverter, calculates the ECM score, and exposes it via a local HTTP endpoint. Every connected SI device polls that endpoint directly — no broker, no cloud, no subscription required for device intelligence.

The ECM (Energy Comfort Model) combines solar surplus, battery state, grid dependence and load demand into a single live score — the shared energy language that every SI device speaks. MQTT is available for the dashboard and Home Assistant, but SI devices use HTTP and work completely independently of any broker.

✦
Live inverter telemetry

Solar PV, load, grid import/export, battery SOC and charge/discharge — polled every few seconds via Modbus RS485.

✦
ECM Score — via local HTTP

A live 0–20 energy comfort score exposed on a local HTTP endpoint. SI devices poll it directly — no broker dependency. MQTT publishes the same data for the dashboard and Home Assistant.

✦
Inverter controls

Set SOC targets per time slot, configure work mode, grid charge and solar sell — all from the app.

✦
Pairs in minutes

Installer provisions via QR code. No port forwarding, no cloud account required for local operation.

SI Pool
Smart pool controller — one per pool

Intelligent pool control, from filtration to heating.

SI Pool replaces the old timer-and-floats world with a calibrated controller that combines temperature sensing, pump control, energy metering, filter health tracking and dry-run protection in a single installer-friendly unit.

SI Pool is the first device to run ECM automation. The pool controller doesn't need to understand your inverter or battery — it simply reads the ECM score and decides whether enough surplus energy exists to run the pump or heat pump. You set the thresholds. SI Pool does the rest.

✦
Temperature monitoring & control

Live pool temp with configurable target — SI Pool manages heating automatically to hit your setpoint.

✦
Pump scheduling & energy metering

Two independent timer windows, live power draw in watts, voltage and current — full visibility of pump energy use.

✦
Filter health & dry-run protection

Filter clean threshold configurable in watts. Dry-run detection with grace period protects the pump from running without water.

✦
Remote control

Turn the pump, light and heat pump on or off from the app — whether you're at home or away.

SI Water
Tank & borehole monitor — one per tank

Know exactly what's in your tank, right now.

SI Water uses a digital RS485 pressure sensor to measure the water column depth continuously. Pressure readings are converted live into volume in litres, height in centimetres and percentage fill — accurate, instant and with no float switches, no moving parts and no guessing.

There are no physical flow sensors. Inflow and outflow are derived in real time from changes in the pressure reading — giving you reliable flow data, usage totals and leak detection without any additional hardware.

✦
Pressure-based level sensing

Digital RS485 pressure sensor measures water column depth — converted live to litres, cm and %, calibrated to your tank dimensions.

✦
Derived flow monitoring

Inflow and outflow calculated live from pressure changes — no flow sensors required. Reliable, accurate and instant.

✦
Usage totals & event log

Today, this month and all-time in/out totals. Recent water events log every fill and draw cycle with volume.

✦
Low-level alerts

Push notification when tank drops below 20% — so you're never caught without water.
