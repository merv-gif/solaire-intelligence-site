What SI Switch does

More solar used. Less wasted. Less bought.

☀️

Maximise Solar Self-Consumption

Automatically runs loads when excess solar is available, reducing reliance on Eskom and improving the return on your solar investment. The load only runs when the energy is already there — no expensive grid top-ups required.

💰

Lower Electricity Bills

Uses available solar energy first. Every kWh consumed from surplus solar is a kWh that doesn't come from the grid — at your current municipal tariff rate. Across a pool pump, geyser or heat pump, that adds up fast.

⚡

Intelligent Energy Decisions

Powered by the SI Energy Comfort Margin (ECM), the switch makes real-time decisions based on your solar production, battery status, household demand and grid conditions — not a fixed timer that doesn't know what the sun is doing.

📱

Full Remote Control

Monitor power consumption, override schedules, switch the load on or off and receive alerts — from anywhere, using the SI Portal app. No VPN, no IT setup.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Receive push alerts when the load starts or stops, when abnormal power draw is detected, or when the device requires attention. Know what's happening without being there.

🛡️

Fail-Safe Local Operation

All automation logic runs on the device itself. If internet connectivity is lost, the switch continues operating against its last-known schedule and ECM state — no cloud dependency for the core function.

🔧

Easy Retrofit Installation

Installs into existing electrical systems without replacing appliances or rewiring. A qualified installer can have it commissioned in under an hour. No new equipment to purchase — your existing load becomes the smart load.

📊

Energy Monitoring

On-board energy metering tracks real power draw and accumulated consumption. Review daily kWh consumed, operating hours and estimated cost savings in the SI Portal app.

Pool Pump Mode

It doesn't just save electricity. It saves your pump and your pool.

Pool Pump Mode turns SI Switch into an active pool health monitor. Most pool owners backwash on a fixed schedule — wasting thousands of litres of water whether the filter needs it or not. SI Switch watches the pump's power draw in real time. A clean filter allows free water flow and the pump draws full wattage. As the filter loads up, flow drops and so does the power. When it falls below your threshold, the app flags it as Dirty. Backwash only when it's actually needed — not before, not after.

💧
Save thousands of litres a year
Backwash only when the filter is actually dirty — not on a guess or a fixed weekly schedule.
🏊
Protect your pool water quality
A blocked filter strains the pump, reduces circulation and compromises water chemistry. Know the moment it needs attention.

Dry Run Protection addresses the number one cause of pool pump failure worldwide. Suction loss, a clogged skimmer basket, a closed valve or a low water level — all result in the pump running without water. Most homeowners only find out when the motor burns out. SI Switch detects near-zero current draw while the pump is running and cuts power before damage occurs. A configurable grace period allows for normal priming so you don't get false trips.

🛡️
Save thousands of rands on pump repairs and replacements
Pool pump replacements routinely run R3,000–R8,000 installed. Dry run is the leading cause. Built-in protection costs nothing extra and runs silently in the background every time the pump is on.

Powered by ECM

Not a timer. An energy decision.

Traditional smart switches run on fixed timers. SI Switch runs on the Energy Comfort Margin (ECM) — a real-time score that combines solar production, battery state of charge, household load and grid conditions into a single number from 0 to 20. When the score is high enough, the load runs. When conditions change, it stops. Automatically.

16–20
Strong surplus — run all loads
8–15
Moderate — priority loads only
0–7
Low — loads suppressed
