Optional Remote Access

Cloud and remote access are additive — never load-bearing. If the internet drops, your pool pump still runs on schedule. Your tank still gets monitored. Your inverter data still logs locally. We call this local-first. In South Africa, we consider it non-negotiable.

Philosophy

Your systems should work whether the cloud does or not.

Most "smart home" products stop being smart the moment the internet drops or the company's server goes down. SI products don't. Every edge device retains autonomous control — locally, without phoning home.

Remote access is available and easy. But it is never required for your systems to function. In South Africa, with unreliable connectivity and rolling load shedding, that is the only responsible design choice.
