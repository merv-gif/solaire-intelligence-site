What SI Water does

Stop guessing. Start knowing.

💧

Precise Tank Level

A stainless-steel submersible pressure transducer sits inside your tank. It knows the exact water level — in litres and percentage — at all times. No floats. No guesswork.

📊

Real-Time Flow Intelligence

Derived inflow and outflow rate in L/min, with idle-state detection. Know when your borehole pump is filling, when you're drawing water, and when something unexpected is flowing.

🔍

Leak Detection

A dripping ball-valve or a burst pipe can waste thousands of litres before you notice. SI Water spots the anomaly and tells you the same day — often before the next water bill.

📅

Usage Accounting

Daily, monthly and lifetime fill and usage totals, exposed as Home Assistant entities. Know your consumption patterns. Catch the slow drain that costs a fortune over a year.

⚙️

Pump & Valve Control

Automate your borehole pump, transfer pump or solenoid valves based on tank level or flow state — via Home Assistant automations that SI Water enables natively.

🛡️

Pump Protection

If the pump is running when it shouldn't, or not running when it should — you'll know immediately. That early warning can easily save the cost of a replacement pump.

🏠

Home Assistant Native

All data lives in Home Assistant via ESPHome. Level, flow, daily totals, alerts — all native HA entities. No custom integrations. No cloud bridge. No subscription.

🇿🇦

Built for SA Conditions

Designed for South African tank infrastructure: boreholes, JoJo tanks, rainwater harvesting, municipal backup. Not a re-labelled European product.
